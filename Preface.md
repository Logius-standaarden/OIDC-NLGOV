## Dutch government Assurance profile for OpenID Connect 1.0

This profile is based upon the [[[iGOV.OpenID]]] as published by the [OpenID Foundation](https://openid.net/foundation/). It should be considered a fork of this profile as the iGov profile is geared more towards the American situation and in the Netherlands we have to deal with an European Union context.

[The Governance of this standard](https://gitdocumentatie.logius.nl/publicatie/api/beheermodel/) is described by the API-Standaarden beheermodel in a [separate repository](https://github.com/Logius-standaarden/API-Standaarden-Beheermodel/) and published by Logius (api@logius.nl).
